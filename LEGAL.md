### What AniFlow is

AniFlow is free software that you run yourself, on your own hardware. It keeps a watchlist of the anime you follow, looks for releases on the feeds and search sites that you add, passes the ones that match your own rules to your own torrent client (qBittorrent, Deluge or Transmission), and files finished downloads into your library. Every rule is yours: AniFlow does not decide what you watch or where you get it from.

### What AniFlow does not do

AniFlow does not host, store, upload or distribute any films, series or other copyrighted works, and it does not include any list of sites, feeds, indexers or links to such works. It does not download the media files itself: the transfer is carried out by your own torrent client. AniFlow may fetch small torrent files or magnet links from the sites you have added, so that it can pass them to your client. The sites, the feeds and the torrents are chosen by you.

### Services AniFlow contacts

To fill in titles, episode counts, release dates and cover images, AniFlow contacts public information services: AniList and Kitsu by default, Jikan, MyAnimeList and Shikimori if you switch them on, AniDB (its published title list, at most once a week) and a public id-mapping list on GitHub. It also contacts the sites you add yourself. Those services receive a request from the computer that runs AniFlow, which includes its network address and what is being looked up. If you set a proxy, the requests go through it. AniFlow is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of these services or their owners; their names and logos belong to them, and their own terms of use apply to your use of them.

### Your data

AniFlow does not collect, analyse or send information about you or about how you use it. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no update check and no account with its author. Your watchlist, settings, passwords and keys stay on your own machine, in the folders you gave it; the settings file keeps them as plain text, so protect that folder. The only things that leave your machine are the requests described above and the requests to services you set up yourself, such as your torrent client or media server. The diagnostics file is created only when you download it, and AniFlow never sends it anywhere. The author of AniFlow receives none of your data.
